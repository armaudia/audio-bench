# Contributing

Software contributions are made under Apache-2.0. Every contributed commit in a
pull request must carry a `Signed-off-by: Name <email>` trailer certifying the
[Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/). Use your
own verified sign-off; the project does not sign for contributors. CI verifies
that every commit has a sign-off, but does not claim to establish legal ownership.

Use a PR, code-owner review and the required CI checks. Declare third-party code,
models, datasets, recordings and licensing exceptions. Do not submit private audio,
weights or secrets. Software contribution approval does not establish data or model
rights; hosted submissions remain closed. See DATA_LICENSE.md.
